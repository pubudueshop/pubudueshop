🔌 Power Supply & Speaker Selection

For best results, use a good-quality regulated DC power supply. A 12V supply is suitable for lower-power applications, while a 19V or 24V DC supply can provide more available output power when the speakers and power supply are suitable.

Use 4Ω–8Ω speakers and connect the Left and Right speakers to their respective output terminals. Because this is a BTL/Class-D amplifier design, the speaker negative terminals should not be connected together or directly to GND. Use the dedicated L+, L-, R+, R- output terminals.

For high-volume operation, choose a power supply with sufficient current capacity. An undersized adapter can cause voltage drop, distortion and reduced output power.
