Delivery Methods

At Pubudu Electronics, we strive to get your orders to you as quickly and reliably as possible. We offer several delivery options across Sri Lanka to suit your needs.

1. Island-wide Courier Delivery

We partner with leading courier services to provide reliable delivery to any corner of Sri Lanka.

  • Delivery Time: 1-3 working days for Western Province, 3-5 days for other areas.
  • Tracking: You will receive a tracking number once your order is dispatched.
  • Cost: Calculated based on weight and destination at checkout.

2. Local Pickup (Weliweriya)

You can choose to pick up your order directly from our location in Weliweriya, Gampaha.

  • Address: Pubudu Electronics, Weliweriya.
  • Timing: Please wait for a confirmation message that your order is ready before visiting.
  • Ready Time: Usually within 2-4 hours.

3. Same Day Delivery (Gampaha District)

For urgent orders within the Gampaha district, we can arrange same-day delivery via local pick-up services or our own staff when available.

  • Availability: Contact us via WhatsApp before 11:00 AM to check availability.
  • Extra Charge: Additional charges may apply based on distance.

Packaging Quality

We take great care in packaging delicate electronic components. All items are securely wrapped in anti-static materials and protective cushioning to ensure they arrive in perfect condition.
