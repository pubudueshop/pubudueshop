SM7015 AC-DC Offline PWM Power Switch IC (730V, SOP-8) – Genuine Replacement Component in Sri Lanka

The SM7015 is a highly integrated, current-mode PWM power switch IC engineered for low-cost, high-reliability offline AC-DC power supplies. Manufactured by Shenzhen Sunmoon Microelectronics, this chip combines a 730V breakdown voltage power MOSFET, a high-voltage startup circuit, and a fixed 60kHz PWM controller inside an 8-pin SOP-8 (SOIC-8) SMD package.

Whether you are an electronics repair technician troubleshooting a dead auxiliary power rail on a kitchen appliance or an engineer designing a compact non-isolated offline power supply, the SM7015 minimizes external component count while delivering stable 12V or 18V regulation across Sri Lanka's 230V AC mains supply.

### Key Features &amp; Benefits

•730V Monolithic High-Voltage Process: Integrated 730V drain-source breakdown MOSFET provides strong voltage surge margin for rectified 230V AC mains environments.


•Wide Universal AC Input: Operates reliably across 85V AC to 265V AC input voltage ranges.


•Pin-Selectable Output Voltage (12V / 18V): Easily configures output voltage via the EN pin (short EN to HVDD for 12V output; leave EN floating for 18V output) without complex external feedback networks.


•Low Standby Power Consumption: Consumes less than 120mW at 220V AC , meeting modern energy-efficiency requirements for standby rails.


•Enhanced EMI / EMC Performance: Incorporates built-in frequency dithering (jitter ±4%) around its 60kHz oscillator frequency along with intelligent soft-drive switching technology to reduce electromagnetic interference.


•Built-In Soft Start: Prevents inrush current spikes and output voltage overshoot during power-up.


•Comprehensive Built-In Protections: Includes Over-Temperature Protection ( OTP at 150°C), Over-Current Protection ( OCP ), Over-Voltage Protection ( OVP ), and Under-Voltage Lockout / Protection ( UVP ).

### Technician Repair &amp; PCB Replacement Notes

1. Check Surrounding Components Before Replacing: When an SM7015 fails due to a mains surge or lightning spike, always inspect the input fusible resistor (typically $10\Omega–22\Omega$ , 1W–2W), primary rectifier diodes (e. g., 1N4007), high-voltage bulk electrolytic capacitor ( $400\text{V}$ ), freewheeling diode, and HVDD capacitor before soldering the new IC.
2. Verify Pin 1 (EN) Configuration: Confirm whether the board ties Pin 1 ( EN ) to Pin 2 ( HVDD ) for 12V operation or leaves it open for 18V operation.
3. Thermal Management: Pins 5, 6, 7, and 8 serve as both the electrical source/ground and the primary thermal heatsink path. Ensure clean solder joints across all four ground pads.
4. Variant Note: Do not confuse SM7015 (730V breakdown, 60kHz, Buck/Buck-Boost) with SM7015NA (500V breakdown, 66. 5kHz, Buck-only). Always match the exact part number printed on your original IC.

Q1: What is the SM7015 IC used for?
A: The SM7015 is an offline AC-DC PWM power switch IC used to step down rectified mains voltage (85V–265V AC) into a regulated 12V or 18V DC supply (up to 150mA at 230V AC). It is most commonly found on the control boards of induction cookers, electric rice cookers, and small home appliances.Q2: How is the output voltage selected on the SM7015?
A: The output voltage is configured using Pin 1 (EN). When Pin 1 (EN) is connected to Pin 2 (HVDD), the circuit outputs 12V. When Pin 1 (EN) is left floating (unconnected), the circuit outputs 18V.Q3: What is the difference between SM7015 and SM7015NA?
A: According to Shenzhen Sunmoon Microelectronics datasheets, the standard SM7015 features a 730V internal MOSFET and operates at 60kHz (supporting Buck and Buck-Boost topologies), whereas the SM7015NA is rated at 500V and switches at 66.5kHz (Buck topology).
