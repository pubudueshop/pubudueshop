Return & Warranty Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Please read our policy regarding returns and warranties for electronic components.

1. General Returns

You may return items within 7 days of purchase if they are in their original, unopened packaging. Please note:

  • Items must be unused and in the same condition as received.
  • Shipping costs for returns are the responsibility of the customer.
  • A small restocking fee might apply in certain cases.

2. Damaged or Defective Items

If you receive an item that is damaged or functionally defective, we will offer a replacement or refund.

  • Please report the issue within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Share clear photos or a short video demonstrating the issue via WhatsApp.
  • We will conduct a basic assessment and process your replacement promptly.

3. Warranty Policy

Electronic components vary in their warranty coverage. Generally:

  • Modules & Sensors: 7-day checking warranty unless specified otherwise.
  • Power Adapters & Tools: 1-3 month warranty against manufacturing defects.
  • Consumables: Soldering wire, flux, etc. are not returnable once used.

4. Exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage caused by:

  • Incorrect wiring or polarities.
  • Over-voltage or short circuits.
  • Physical damage or modification by the user.
  • Water or moisture damage.

For any return requests, please contact our support team with your order details.
