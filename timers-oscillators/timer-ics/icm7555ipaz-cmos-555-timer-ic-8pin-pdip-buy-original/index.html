Product Description

The ICM7555IPAZ (manufactured by Renesas Electronics / Intersil) is a high-performance, ultra-low-power CMOS General Purpose RC Timer Integrated Circuit housed in a standard 8-Lead Plastic Dual In-Line Package (8-PDIP). Engineered as a pin-for-pin, direct drop-in upgrade for conventional bipolar NE555, SE555, and TLC555 timers in most circuit designs, the ICM7555IPAZ delivers dramatically reduced quiescent supply current (60 µA typical), an ultra-wide operating supply voltage range (2 V to 18 V), and high-speed switching up to 1 MHz.

Unlike legacy bipolar 555 timers that generate large supply current spikes ("crowbar" currents) during output transitions, the CMOS architecture of the ICM7555IPAZ eliminates switching transients on the power rail and removes the need for an external decoupling capacitor on the Control Voltage (Pin 5) for stable operation. Its ultra-low input bias currents (20 pA typical at Trigger, Threshold, and Reset pins) allow engineers and repair technicians to use much higher-value timing resistors and smaller, lower-cost capacitors to achieve precision time delays ranging from microseconds up to several hours.

Important Technician &amp; Replacement Notes

1. Direct NE555 / TLC555 Upgrade: The ICM7555IPAZ is pin-compatible with standard 8-pin NE555 , LM555 , SE555 , and TLC555 timers. However, note that while a bipolar NE555 can sink/source up to ~200 mA directly (e. g., to drive a heavy relay coil without a driver transistor), the CMOS ICM7555IPAZ is optimized for low power and logic/MOSFET driving (output sink/source ratings are specified in the tens of milliamps). Always check load current if replacing a bipolar 555 that directly drives a high-current coil.
2. Multiple-Supply Systems: Per Renesas datasheet guidelines, in circuits using multiple power rails, the $V_{DD}$ supply to the ICM7555IPAZ should be powered on first before applying external input signals to prevent latch-up.
3. Soldering Precaution: As a Pb-free PDIP through-hole package, it is designed for standard wave soldering or temperature-controlled manual soldering irons—do not subject PDIP packages to SMD reflow oven profiles.
