Privacy Policy

At Pubudu Electronics, accessible from ichouse.lk, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Pubudu Electronics and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you place an order or contact us. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, delivery address, phone number, and email address.
  • Order Details: Products purchased, quantities, and transaction date.
  • Communication Records: Messages sent via WhatsApp or email.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Process and deliver your orders.
  • Communicate with you regarding order status or technical support.
  • Improve and personalize your shopping experience.
  • Send you updates or promotional offers (only if you have opted in).
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our website.

3. Data Protection

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible by authorized personnel involved in fulfilling your orders.

4. Third-Party Services

We may share your information with trusted third-party services only to the extent necessary to perform their services, such as:

  • Courier Services: To deliver your orders to your doorstep.
  • Firebase (Google): Our backend service for managing order data.

5. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like many other websites, Pubudu Electronics uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, or to request that we correct or delete your information. To do so, please contact us via WhatsApp.

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
