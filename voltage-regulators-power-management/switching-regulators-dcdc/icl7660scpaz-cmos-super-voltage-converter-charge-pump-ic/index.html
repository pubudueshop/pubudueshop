ICL7660SCPAZ – Monolithic CMOS Super Voltage Converter IC (8-Pin PDIP)

The ICL7660SCPAZ (manufactured by Renesas Electronics / Intersil) is a high-efficiency monolithic CMOS switched-capacitor Super Voltage Converter housed in a standard through-hole 8-pin PDIP (Plastic Dual In-Line Package). Designed as an enhanced, drop-in upgrade to the industry-standard ICL7660, it converts a positive input supply voltage (+1.5V to +12V) into a corresponding negative output voltage (-1.5V to -12V) using only two external non-critical capacitors and no inductors.

Ideal for op-amp dual-rail supplies, RS-232 interfaces, instrumentation, and portable battery-powered circuits, the ICL7660SCPAZ features a dedicated Frequency Boost pin (Pin 1), lower quiescent supply current, and built-in SCR latch-up protection.

Key Features &amp; Advantages

•Wide Input Supply Voltage Range: Operates from 1.5V to 12V (3. 0V to 12V with LV pin open; 1. 5V to 3. 5V with LV pin tied to GND).


•Minimal External Components: Requires only two external capacitors (charge pump and charge reservoir)—no bulky inductors needed.


•Frequency Boost Pin (Pin 1): Connecting Pin 1 to $V^+$ increases the internal oscillator frequency from 10 kHz to 35 kHz (typical) , allowing smaller capacitors and lower output ripple.


•High Conversion Efficiency: Delivers 99% minimum open-circuit voltage conversion efficiency and 96% minimum power efficiency .


•No External Diode Required: Eliminates the external protection diode needed on older ICL7660 versions across the full temperature and voltage range while remaining backward-compatible with legacy PCB layouts.


•Lead-Free &amp; RoHS Compliant: The -Z suffix confirms a 100% Matte Tin Pb-free, RoHS-compliant PDIP package.

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | BOOST | Frequency Boost input. Leave open/GND for ~10 kHz operation; connect to $V^+$ to increase switching frequency to ~35 kHz. |
| 2 | CAP+ | Positive terminal for the external charge-pump flying capacitor ($C_1$). |
| 3 | GND | Circuit ground reference pin. |
| 4 | CAP- | Negative terminal for the external charge-pump flying capacitor ($C_1$). |
| 5 | VOUT | Negative voltage output ($V_{OUT} = -V^+$ in standard inverter mode). |
| 6 | LV | Low-Voltage operation select. Tie to GND when $V^+ &lt; 3.5\text{V}$; leave floating (open) when $V^+ \ge 3.5\text{V}$. |

Common Applications

•Dual-Rail Op-Amp Power Supplies: Generating $\pm 5\text{V}$ or $\pm 9\text{V}$ rails from a single positive battery or logic supply.


•Audio Preamp &amp; Guitar Pedal Circuits: Supplying clean negative bias rails in analog audio effects and mixers without a center-tapped transformer.


•RS-232 Serial Communication Interfaces: Providing negative transceiver supply voltages from a single 5V rail.


•Data Acquisition &amp; Instrumentation Systems: Powering analog-to-digital converters (ADCs), sensors, and LCD contrast bias circuits.


•Voltage Doubling &amp; Splitting: Configuring as a positive voltage doubler ( $V_{OUT} \approx 2V^+ - V_D$ ) or precision voltage splitter ( $V_{OUT} = \pm V_S / 2$ ).

Technician &amp; Design Notes

•LV Pin Rule (Pin 6): Always leave Pin 6 (LV) unconnected (floating) when operating above 3.5V to prevent internal latch-up. Tie Pin 6 to GND only when the input supply is between 1.5V and 3.5V .


•Capacitor Selection: Standard 10 µF low-ESR electrolytic or tantalum capacitors work reliably for $C_1$ (pump) and $C_2$ (reservoir) at the default 10 kHz switching frequency.


•Soldering Note: As a Pb-free PDIP package, the ICL7660SCPAZ is rated for manual through-hole soldering or wave soldering only (not reflow oven processing).
