Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about shopping at Pubudu Electronics.

Do you offer island-wide delivery?
Yes! We provide reliable delivery to all districts in Sri Lanka via professional courier services.
How can I track my order?
Once your order is dispatched, we will send you a tracking number via WhatsApp/Email which you can use on the courier service's website.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Bank Transfers, LANKAQR, and Cash on Delivery. For more details, visit our Payment Methods page.
Are the items tested before shipping?
Yes, we perform basic functionality checks on most items like modules and sensors before packaging to ensure you receive working components.
Can I pick up my order from your store?
Absolutely! You can select 'Local Pickup' at checkout and visit us in Weliweriya. Please wait for the confirmation message that your order is ready.
Do you provide technical support?
Yes, we offer expert technical support to our customers via WhatsApp. Feel free to contact us if you need help with your projects or components.
