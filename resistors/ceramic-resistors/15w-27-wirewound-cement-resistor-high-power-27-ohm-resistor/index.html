Product Description

The 15W 2.7Ω wirewound cement resistor is a high-power electronic component designed for circuits that require reliable resistance with excellent heat dissipation. Built with a मजबूत ceramic (cement) casing, this resistor is ideal for handling high temperatures and maintaining stable performance under heavy load conditions. Its compact rectangular design with sturdy leads makes it easy to mount on PCBs or chassis.

This resistor features a resistance value of 2.7 ohms (Ω) with a power rating of 15 watts, making it suitable for applications where high current flow is involved. The wirewound construction ensures precision, low noise, and better durability compared to standard carbon resistors. It also offers good tolerance stability and resistance to environmental stress such as heat and vibration.

Commonly used in power supplies, audio amplifiers, motor control circuits, and industrial electronics, this resistor helps limit current, divide voltage, and protect sensitive components. It is also widely used in DIY electronics and repair projects.
