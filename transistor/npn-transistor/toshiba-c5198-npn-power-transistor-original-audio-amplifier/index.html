Product Description

The Toshiba C5198 (2SC5198) NPN Power Transistor is a high-performance audio power transistor widely used in professional audio amplifiers, home theater systems, PA amplifiers, and high-power electronic circuits. Designed for excellent linearity, high current handling, and reliable thermal performance, the C5198 is a trusted choice for amplifier repair technicians and electronics enthusiasts in Sri Lanka.

This transistor is commonly paired with the 2SA1941 (A1941) complementary PNP transistor to create powerful Class-AB audio amplifier output stages. Its robust TO-3P package allows efficient heat dissipation, making it suitable for demanding high-power applications.
