Product Description

**Reliable 30A PWM Solar Charge Controller for 12V & 24V Solar Systems
**
Looking for a dependable 30A Solar Charge Controller in Sri Lanka? This 30A PWM Solar Charge Controller (Model: W88-C) is designed to efficiently manage solar panel charging and protect your battery system from overcharging, deep discharge, short circuits, and reverse polarity. It is ideal for home solar systems, solar lighting projects, battery charging stations, RVs, caravans, and off-grid solar installations.

The controller automatically detects 12V or 24V battery systems, making installation simple and convenient. Its built-in LCD display provides real-time monitoring of battery voltage, charging status, load status, and system parameters. The integrated USB ports allow you to charge mobile phones, tablets, and other USB-powered devices directly from your solar system.

Technical Details

This PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) controller optimizes battery charging by regulating charging current from the solar panel to the battery. The intelligent microcontroller system continuously monitors battery condition and adjusts charging parameters automatically to improve battery life and system stability.

The controller supports various battery types including sealed lead-acid, AGM, gel, and deep-cycle batteries commonly used in solar power systems across Sri Lanka.
