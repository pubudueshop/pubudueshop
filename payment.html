Payment Methods

We provide a variety of secure and convenient payment options for our customers in Sri Lanka.

1. Bank Transfer (Commercial Bank & HNB)

Our preferred payment method for all orders. You can transfer funds directly to our bank accounts via online banking or cash deposit.

  • Bank Name: Commercial Bank / HNB
  • Branch: Weliweriya
  • Confirmation: Please share a screenshot or copy of the bank slip via WhatsApp for instant confirmation.

2. LANKAQR (Easy Payment)

Pay instantly using any bank's QR payment mobile app by scanning our LANKAQR code. It's fast, secure, and fee-free.

3. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Available for selected regions and courier partners. Pay in cash directly to the delivery agent when your order arrives.

4. KOKO / Mintpay (Coming Soon)

We are currently integrating Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL) services like KOKO and Mintpay to allow you to split your payments into interest-free installments.

Security Guarantee

We take your payment security seriously. All transactions are handled with the highest standards of safety. Your data is protected, and we never store your delicate financial information on our servers.
