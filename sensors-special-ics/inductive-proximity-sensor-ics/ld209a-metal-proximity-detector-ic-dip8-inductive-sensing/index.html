Product Description

The LD209A (functionally equivalent to the CS209A) is a bipolar monolithic integrated circuit designed for metal detection and inductive proximity sensing applications. Shown here in a through-hole DIP-8 package (matching the provided unit photos), the IC integrates dual on-chip current regulators, an oscillator with variable low-level feedback, a negative-envelope peak demodulator, a comparator with hysteresis, and dual complementary open-collector output stages.

When paired with an external LC resonant tank circuit, the LD209A monitors changes in the quality factor (Q) of the inductor as metallic objects approach. A built-in transient suppression network helps absorb negative voltage spikes at the tank terminal. Ideal for electronics technicians, repair workshops, automation builders, and engineering projects across Sri Lanka looking for a dedicated inductive proximity sensing IC.

Key Features

  • Inductive Proximity &amp; Metal Sensing Architecture: Detects Q-factor loading on an external LC tank circuit when metal enters the sensing field.
  • Dual On-Chip Current Regulators: Includes a separate current regulator for the oscillator to stabilize operation.
  • Variable Low-Level Feedback: Maintains oscillation during low-Q operating conditions.
  • Negative Transient Suppression: Protects against negative transients occurring at the LC tank pin.
  • Complementary Open-Collector Outputs (OUT1 &amp; OUT2): Provides opposite-state open-collector outputs (external pull-up/load resistors required).
  • Adjustable Detection Range: Sensing threshold/range can be tuned via an external feedback resistor/potentiometer between OSC and RF .

Typical Applications

  • Inductive proximity switches and non-contact metal sensors
  • Industrial &amp; workshop metal detection circuits and prototypes
  • Object/part presence sensing on small conveyors or fixtures
  • Security, access, or counting circuits triggered by metallic targets
  • Replacement &amp; repair in legacy/custom analog proximity sensor boards

Practical Buying &amp; Technical Notes for Sri Lankan Technicians

  • Confirm Package &amp; Pinout: The photos show a through-hole DIP-8 part marked LD209A (with batch/lot code DHAMIB on the top surface). Double-check your PCB footprint and pin numbers before ordering.
  • Tank &amp; Tuning Sensitivity: Actual sensing distance depends heavily on the Q factor of your external inductor and capacitor, core geometry/loss, target metal type (e. g., carbon steel vs. stainless steel vs. aluminum/copper), and the OSC – RF feedback resistance.
  • Output Loads: Because OUT1 and OUT2 are open-collector outputs, ensure proper external pull-up or load circuitry is wired according to your supply voltage and current requirements.
